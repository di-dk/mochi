description: Nepal facts and formats: Bikram Sambat dates, lakh/crore money, local units, festivals, time

Use for dates, prices, land or grain measures and everyday facts about Nepal.
1. Calendar: Nepal's official calendar is Bikram Sambat (BS), about 56 years 8 months ahead of AD; the year starts in
   mid-April (Baisakh 1). Months: बैशाख, जेठ, असार, साउन, भदौ, असोज, कात्तिक, मंसिर, पुष, माघ, फागुन, चैत. Week:
   आइतबार (Sunday) first; शनिबार (Saturday) is the weekly holiday. ALWAYS use nepali_date for conversions and for
   "today": month lengths (29-32 days) change every year, so never calculate a BS date yourself. Write dates as
   "<year> <month> <day>, <weekday>" with Devanagari digits for Nepali and give both AD and BS when it matters
   (contracts, visas, forms).
2. Time: Nepal Standard Time is UTC+5:45.
3. Money: Nepali rupee (रु., Rs., NPR). Group digits the Nepali way: 12,34,567 (lakh = 1,00,000; crore = 1,00,00,000;
   अर्ब = 100 crore). nepali_format mode=money gives "रु. १२,३४,५६७".
4. Units (approximate, say so): land in the hills 1 रोपनी = 16 आना = 64 पैसा (about 509 m2); in the Terai 1 बिघा = 20
   कट्ठा = 400 धुर (about 6,773 m2); grain: मुरी, पाथी, माना; gold: तोला (about 11.66 g). Convert with calculations,
   show the arithmetic.
5. Festivals follow lunar calendars and move every year (दशैं, तिहार, होली, तीज, बुद्ध जयन्ती, माघे संक्रान्ति,
   नयाँ वर्ष). Never state a festival date from memory: web_search it (hamropatro.com lists them) and give the BS and
   AD date from the source, then check it with nepali_date.
6. Basics: capital काठमाडौं; Nepali is the official language (Devanagari); many other languages are spoken
   (Maithili, Bhojpuri, Tharu, Tamang, Newar...). For anything current (prices, rules, news) search the web first.
For the language itself, see the skill nepali.
